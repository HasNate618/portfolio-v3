# Nathan Espejo

Most of what I build lately is LLM tooling I use every day, and I put as much care into the parts around the model as the model itself, with tests running in CI, data that syncs cleanly and changes I can roll back. I'd rather pick a cheap model that does the job well than pay for the biggest one.

Right now I'm using Campus, the AI agent I built that reads all six of my courses, and running a homelab of two Linux (NixOS) machines managed from a single repo, where every change is declared in config, reviewed and easy to roll back.

Outside of AI I build for fun: games and 3D in Unity and Blender, VR, and hardware with Arduino and ESP32.
