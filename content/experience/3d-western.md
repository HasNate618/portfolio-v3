# VP of Data/ML (promoted from Software Developer), 3D Western

Nathan joined 3D Western as a Software Developer in Sep 2025 and was promoted to VP of Data/ML, finishing in Aug 2026. His work built the club's print-approval dataset and benchmark, which decides whether a submitted 3D model is safe to print.

## About 3D Western

3D Western is a student-run organization at Western University that provides free 3D printing. Its printers sit in Western's makerspace in the entrepreneurship building, where entrepreneurs can test products.

## Leading the data team

His VP term (May to Aug 2026) ran alongside his TeraGo internship. As VP he led two developers through GitHub tickets and PR review, testing their changes locally and integrating them into the shared pipeline.

## Dataset

He built a labelled dataset from 4,127 scraped 3D models, rendered in Blender and class-checked by a VLM.

## Benchmark

He benchmarked base vision models on the 2,559 clean renders, with safe, unsafe and grey classes, as groundwork for future fine-tuning. The best scored 84.2% accuracy.

## Earlier work as Software Developer

From Sep 2025 to Apr 2026 he worked on the Dockerized print pipeline that automates slicing, orientation, support generation and printability checks, and wrote the scraping and rendering scripts that produce labelled image datasets.

## Stack

Python, Blender, VLMs, Docker, Linux, rendering automation, model evaluation.
